Distribution research

These works are shown as part of an ongoing study of how immersive storytelling can reach audiences in this region. There isn't yet a clear, sustainable economic model for screening independent VR stories here, so current screenings run on trust, good faith and field building, always with the artists' consent. We work from a grounded knowledge of what these works cost and earn in other markets, and the aim is a model that values artists properly here too.
